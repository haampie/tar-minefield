N31-pax-linkpath-not-utf8: must reject

A pax `linkpath` that is not UTF-8.

The archive has a symlink `link`, whose pax header has `linkpath=caf\xe9`, `café` in Latin-1, and
whose header has the target `from-header`.

POSIX says that pax records are UTF-8, apart from names under
`hdrcharset=BINARY`, which this header does not have. This case is ambiguous because
the target is the bytes as they are, `caf\xe9`; a decoding of them, such as `café` or `caf` followed
by U+FFFD; the header's `from-header`, to a reader that drops a record it cannot decode; or none, as
the archive is invalid.

Decision point: what a pax value that is not UTF-8 means.
Seen in: none of the scanned tarballs.
See also: N32, the same target in the header, and N24, a pax `path` that is not UTF-8.
