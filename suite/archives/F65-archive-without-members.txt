F65-archive-without-members: must accept, and extract its tree

An archive without members: only the two zero blocks that end it.

The archive has the end-of-archive marker, two zero blocks, without a member before it.

In POSIX, an archive ends with two zero blocks, and it is not said that a member comes before
them. GNU tar writes such an archive for an empty list of files, and RubyGems for a gem without
files. The tree is empty.

Seen in: 38 gems that do not have files, such as lil 0.0.4 and slds 0.1.1.
See also: F66, a file of 0 bytes, which is rejected.

0x000000  2 zero blocks: the end-of-archive marker
0x000400  end of file
