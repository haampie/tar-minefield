N25-symlink-empty-target: must reject

A symlink with an empty target.

The archive has one symlink, `link`, whose linkname field is empty.

A symlink to the empty string cannot be created on most systems. POSIX allows it: the
contents of a symlink are stored in its linkname field, and `symlink()` takes them as a
string, not as a pathname; where the system refuses to create it, pax
reports that it cannot create the link. This case is ambiguous in practice because
readers fail, make an empty file, skip the member, or make a link to `.`.

Decision point: whether a link with an empty target is a link.
Seen in: none of the scanned tarballs.
