F31-pax-xattr-not-utf8: must accept, and extract its tree

An xattr in a pax header whose value is not UTF-8, as libarchive and star write them.

POSIX says that pax records are UTF-8, apart from names under `hdrcharset=BINARY`. But
xattrs are bytes, and libarchive and star write them in pax headers: libarchive as both
`LIBARCHIVE.xattr.*` (in base64) and `SCHILY.xattr.*` (as they are), and star as `SCHILY.xattr.*`.
Here `SCHILY.xattr.user.data` is `\xff\xfe`. xattrs are not part of the tree, and implementations
that do not extract them can skip them without decoding them, so every implementation must extract
`a`.

Seen in: 31 release tarballs, such as R 4.5.2, Minion 10.31 and keepassxc 2.7.8, all in
`SCHILY.xattr.*`. No tarball scanned for it has another pax value that is not UTF-8, apart from
names.
