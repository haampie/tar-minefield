F39-format-pax-after-v7: must reject

A v7 header first, then a member with a pax header: is the `x` header a header, or a file?

The archive has a file `a` whose header has no magic, as in the v7 format, then a pax header with
`path=from-pax`, then a file whose header has the name `from-header`. The pax header and the header
after it have the POSIX magic.

A reader detects the format of an archive, but POSIX does not say from which headers.
A reader that decides it once, from the first header, reads a v7 archive, in which typeflag `x` is
not defined: it extracts the pax header as a regular file, `PaxHeader`, which holds
the records, and the member as `from-header`. A reader that looks at the magic of every header
applies the pax header, and extracts the member as `from-pax`. This case is ambiguous because the
tree has `a`, `PaxHeader` and `from-header`, or `a` and `from-pax`.

Decision point: whether the format of an archive is decided once, by its first header, or for every
header.
Seen in: none of the scanned tarballs.
