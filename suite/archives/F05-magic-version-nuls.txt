F05-magic-version-nuls: tolerated: a strict parser may reject it; if it accepts it, it must extract its tree, and the default policy accepts it

The POSIX magic with NULs for its version, `ustar\0\0\0`, as some Java archivers write.

The archive has one file, `a`, whose magic is `ustar\0` and whose version field is two NULs.

In POSIX, the version field is `00`.

Seen in: commons-lang 2.6, apache-solr 3.6.2 and commons-logging 1.2.
