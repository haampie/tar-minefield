F79-number-uid-gid-empty: must accept, and extract its tree

Uid and gid fields without digits, as in most crates.

The archive has a file `a` with GNU's magic, whose uid and gid fields are 8 NULs each.

In POSIX, the uid and gid fields are the owner and the group of a file, which are not
part of the tree, and as numeric fields they are octal numbers, which an empty field
is not. Some writers write them without digits, and a reader takes an empty field for 0, or does not
read it.

Seen in: 5394 of the scanned tarballs, most of them crates.
