N40-pax-global-linkpath: must reject

A global pax header with a `linkpath`.

The archive starts with a global header with `linkpath=global-target`, then has the symlinks `l` to
`header-target-l` and `m` to `header-target-m`.

In POSIX, a global header applies to every later member, and a `linkpath`
overrides the linkname field, so both symlinks point to `global-target`. This case is
ambiguous in practice because, with the global `linkpath` ignored, the symlinks keep their own
targets.

Decision point: whether a global `linkpath` applies.
Seen in: none of the scanned tarballs.
See also: N21, a global pax header with a `path`.
