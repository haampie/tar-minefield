F47-magic-gnu-version-00: must reject

A prefix in a header with GNU's magic and the version `00`: part of the name, or not?

The archive has one file, whose header has GNU's magic, `ustar` and a space, the version `00`, the
name `a` and the prefix `p`.

The magic field is the specification of the format that the archive was written in. In the
ustar format, whose magic is `ustar` and a NUL, and whose version is `00`, the pathname
is the prefix, a slash and the name. GNU's format has the magic `ustar` and a space, the
version of a space and a NUL, and other fields where ustar has its prefix. This case is ambiguous
because the header has the magic of one format and the version of the other, so that the file is
`p/a`, read as ustar, or `a`, read as GNU's format or as a v7 header, which has no prefix.

Decision point: which magic and version make the prefix part of the name.
Seen in: none of the scanned tarballs.
See also: N04, a name split by the ustar prefix, and N11, a GNU header with times where ustar has
its prefix.
