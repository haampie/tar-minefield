F56-type-contiguous: tolerated: a strict parser may reject it; if it accepts it, it must extract its tree, and the default policy accepts it

A contiguous file, typeflag `7`.

The archive has one member, `a`, of typeflag `7`, whose data is `a` and a newline.

In POSIX, typeflag `7` is reserved for a file with some high-performance attribute, and
implementations without such extensions should treat it as a regular file. A reader
that does not recognize typeflag `7` extracts it as a regular file too. This case is
tolerated because that is its one reading: as a regular file, or as a contiguous file where they are
supported, the file is `a` with its data. A strict parser may reject it, as it may any typeflag it
does not support; a reader that skips such a typeflag extracts nothing.

Seen in: none of the scanned tarballs.
See also: F16, an unknown typeflag with a size.
