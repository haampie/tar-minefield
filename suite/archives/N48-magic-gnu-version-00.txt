N48-magic-gnu-version-00: must reject

A prefix in a header with the magic `"ustar "` and the version `"00"`: part of the name, or not?

The archive has one file, whose header has the name `a`, the prefix `p`, the magic `"ustar "` and
the version `"00"`.

The ustar format defines the pathname as the prefix, followed by `/`, followed by the
name. GNU's format has a very similar magic, but it does not have a prefix field: in GNU's own
definition, its magic is the 8 bytes `"ustar \0"`, in place of ustar's magic and version. This case
is ambiguous because the 8 bytes of this header, `"ustar 00"`, are of neither format: a reader that
checks the magic before the prefix extracts `a`, as a GNU or a v7 header, and one that reads the
prefix regardless extracts `p/a`.

Decision point: which magic and version make the prefix part of the name.
Seen in: none of the scanned tarballs.
See also: N04, a name split by the ustar prefix, and N11, a GNU header with times where ustar has
its prefix.
