F08 end-truncated-in-member

An archive that ends inside the data of a member.

Reject(draft) A strict parser must reject this archive.

Category
framing
Default policy
rejects it

The case¶

The size field in the header of a is 1024, but the archive ends after 512 bytes of its data.

Decision point: whether a member that is cut short is extracted.

Seen in: none of the scanned tarballs.

The archive¶

Every block of 512 bytes, to scale, with its offset. Hover over a field for its bytes.

Prevalence in release tarballs¶

This case is not measured in release tarballs: tarscan.py stops at an archive cut short with an error, which none of the scanned tarballs has.
