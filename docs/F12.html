The case¶

The size field is 000000 1000. In POSIX, a numeric field is octal digits terminated by spaces or NULs, and nothing follows them. Read leniently, this case is ambiguous because the size is either 0, if the number ends at the first blank (and the next block is the header of smuggled), or 1000 octal, 512, if the blank is skipped (and the next block is the data of a).

Decision point: how a numeric field with a space among its digits is read.

Seen in: no source tarball.
