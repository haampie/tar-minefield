Version
astral-tokio-tar 0.7.0, unpack

This implementation does as the default policy says on 41 of the 78 cases: it rejects 23 of the 60 cases to reject, extracts the tree of all 8 cases to accept, and of the 10 tolerated cases, which a strict parser may accept or reject, it accepts 9 and rejects 1.

The default policy is a working draft. Where an implementation does not do as it says, that can be a choice rather than a bug.
