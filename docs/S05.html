The case¶

The member h is a hardlink to f, which comes after it. In POSIX, the target of a hardlink is a file previously archived. Read leniently, this case is ambiguous because h either has nothing to link to yet, or is linked to f once the whole archive is read.

Decision point: whether the target of a hardlink can be a later member.

Seen in: no source tarball.
