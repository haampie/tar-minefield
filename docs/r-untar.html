R untar, internal

Version
utils::untar of R 4.6.2, tar = "internal"

This implementation follows the default policy in 75 of 188 cases:

  • it rejects 30 of the 133 cases to reject
  • it extracts the expected tree in 26 of the 36 cases to accept
  • it accepts 16 and rejects 3 of the 19 tolerated cases

The default policy is a working draft. Where an implementation does not do as it says, that can be a choice rather than a bug.

On the other 113, it does not:
