F63 end-unpadded

An archive that ends after the data of a file, before the end of its block.

Reject(draft) A strict parser must reject this archive.

The case¶

The archive has a file a of 5 bytes, and ends right after those bytes: the block of its data is not padded to 512 bytes, and zero blocks do not follow.

In POSIX, an archive is a series of blocks of 512 bytes, and the data of a file takes as many blocks as its size needs. So this archive is cut short, although every byte of a is in it. This case is ambiguous in practice because a reader that reads the data by its size extracts a with its 5 bytes, and one that reads whole blocks does not get the last one, and extracts an empty a, or nothing. Most readers report an error, also of those that extract the 5 bytes, and some extract them without one.

Decision point: whether a member whose last block is not complete is extracted.

Seen in: not measured, as earlier versions of the scanner took such an archive for one without an end-of-archive marker: at most 22 of the scanned tarballs.
