F01 number-leading-space

Numeric fields padded with spaces rather than zeros, as old tars wrote them.

Tolerate(draft) A strict parser may reject this archive. If it accepts it, it must extract the tree below.

The case¶

The mode, uid, gid, size and mtime fields are padded with spaces, such as 7 for the size of a. In POSIX, numeric fields are leading zero-filled octal numbers.

The tree to extract:

pathkindcontents
afile7 bytes, sha256 e47fbedb

Seen in: hundreds of tarballs, many from CPAN: the same as F32, whose checksum is padded too.

The archive¶

Every block of 512 bytes, to scale, with its offset. Hover over a field for its bytes.

Every block, as text 
F01-number-leading-space: tolerated: a strict parser may reject it; if it accepts it, it must extract its tree, and the default policy accepts it
