The case¶

POSIX's ustar header ends at byte 500, with a prefix field of 155 bytes, which a prefix may fill without a NUL. star's own format, xstar, puts tar\0 at offset 508, in bytes that POSIX leaves undefined, and splits the prefix field: 131 bytes for the prefix, then the access and change times, in octal. star itself ends its prefixes with a NUL at byte 130, so that ustar readers read them alike. Here the prefix fills all 155 bytes: 131 ps, then 15000000000 15000000000 , which reads as two times. This case is ambiguous because the prefix is either the whole field, as POSIX defines it, or its first 131 bytes, as readers that recognize tar\0 take it: a is then under a directory whose name ends in those numbers, or of the 131 ps alone.

Decision point: whether tar\0 at offset 508 makes the prefix field shorter.

Seen in: berkeley-db 18.1.40 and ncurses 5.9, which are in star's format; tarscan.py does not record whether a prefix runs past 130 bytes.
