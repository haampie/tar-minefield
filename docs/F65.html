F65 archive-without-members

An archive without members: only the two zero blocks that end it.

Accept(draft) Every implementation must accept this archive, and extract the expected tree below.

The case¶

The archive has the end-of-archive marker, two zero blocks, without a member before it.

In POSIX, an archive ends with two zero blocks, and a member before them is not required. GNU tar writes such an archive for an empty list of files, and RubyGems for a gem without files. The tree is empty.

The expected tree:

pathkindcontents

Seen in: 38 gems that do not have files, such as lil 0.0.4 and slds 0.1.1.

Every block of 512 bytes, to scale, with its offset. Hover over a field for its bytes.
