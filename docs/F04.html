The case¶

POSIX requires two zero blocks at the end of an archive. Without them, an archive cannot be told apart from one cut short after a member.

Decision point: whether an archive without an end-of-archive marker is complete.

Seen in: SDL2_image 2.8.8, ruby 4.0.5 and fastcap 2.0-18Sep92.
