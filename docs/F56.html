F56 number-size-base256

A size of 512 in base-256.

The case¶

The archive has a file a of 512 bytes, whose size field is in base-256: the byte 0x80, followed by 512 as a binary number.

Sizes of 8 GiB and over do not fit in the 11 octal digits of the size field. In pax, they are written in a size record. In GNU's format, they are written in base-256. The first byte of the field, 0x80, tells it apart from octal. In POSIX, numeric fields are octal, so base-256 is an extension. This case is tolerated because it has one reading, here 512: a reader that does not support base-256 cannot take it for octal. It is a small instance of the base-256 sizes of large files, which no case can contain.
