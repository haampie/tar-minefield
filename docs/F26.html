The case¶

A pax size is decimal, and POSIX does not forbid leading zeros, so the size is 777. But a reader that converts with C's strtoul(s, NULL, 0), or that knows the header's octal numbers, reads 0777 as octal, 511. This case is ambiguous in practice because the size is 777, in decimal, 511, in octal, or the header's, 0. The data of a then takes two blocks, one, after which smuggled-2 is a member, or none, and both smuggled-1 and smuggled-2 are.

Decision point: whether a pax number with a leading zero is decimal or octal.

Seen in: no source tarball.
