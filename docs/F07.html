The case¶

The archive ends with two zero blocks, as it should, and is followed by a second archive with a file hidden. In POSIX, the archive ends at the marker, and hidden is not part of it. This case is ambiguous in practice because reading on past the marker, as GNU tar does with --ignore-zeros, extracts hidden as well.

Decision point: whether an implementation stops at the end-of-archive marker.

Seen in: glibmm 2.16.0, which is the whole archive twice, jmol 14.8.0 and commons-jexl 2.1.1.
