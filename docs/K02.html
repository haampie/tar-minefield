The case¶

The header has typeflag 0 and the linkname target. POSIX gives a linkname meaning only for links. This case is ambiguous because the member is either a file, by its typeflag, or a link to target, by its linkname.

Decision point: whether a linkname makes a member a link.

Seen in: bgen 1.1.7.
