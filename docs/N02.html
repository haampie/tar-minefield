N02 name-fills-field

A name of exactly 100 bytes, which fills the name field without a NUL.

The case¶

The name field is 100 ns, without a NUL. In POSIX, the name field ends with a NUL except when all of its bytes are non-NUL, so the name is all 100 bytes.

Every block of 512 bytes, to scale, with its offset. Hover over a field for its bytes.
