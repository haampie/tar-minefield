This implementation follows the default policy in 55 of 78 cases:

  • it rejects 37 of the 60 cases to reject
  • it extracts the tree of all 8 cases to accept
  • it accepts 8 and rejects 2 of the 10 tolerated cases, which a strict parser may accept or reject

The default policy is a working draft. Where an implementation does not do as it says, that can be a choice rather than a bug.
